# 🎨 NeuralBrush — Turn Your Photos into Art

NeuralBrush takes any photo and repaints it in the style of a famous artwork — instantly. Upload a picture, pick an artistic style, and get back a beautifully reimagined image, as if it were painted by the artist themselves.

No design skills. No editing software. No waiting around. Just upload, choose, and download.

## 💡 What Can You Do With It?

- **Personalize your photos** — turn a regular selfie or travel photo into something that looks hand-painted
- **Create unique gifts** — stylized portraits make for memorable, one-of-a-kind presents
- **Design content for social media** — stand out with art-style visuals instead of filters
- **Experiment with art styles** — try the same photo across multiple artistic styles in seconds and compare
- **Adjust the intensity** — control how subtle or dramatic the style transformation looks, so you can dial it in exactly how you want

---

## 🖥️ How to Use It

1. **Open the app** in your browser
2. **Upload your photo** — this is the image you want to transform
3. **Choose a style** — pick from a gallery of ready-made artworks, or upload your own style image
4. **Adjust the strength** (optional) — control how much of the style comes through
5. **Click "Transfer Style"** — your stylized image is generated in just a few seconds
6. **Download and share** your new artwork

It's that simple — no technical knowledge needed.

---

## 🌟 Why It's Different

Most online filters apply the same flat effect to every photo. NeuralBrush is powered by a deep learning model that actually *understands* the painting — its colors, textures, and brush patterns — and intelligently blends that style with your photo's content, rather than just slapping a filter on top.

The result feels less like an Instagram filter and more like a genuine reinterpretation of your photo, the way an artist might paint it.
